FAQ

Questions we often hear.

Can’t see yours? Call +255 786 266 441 or email info@fdsc.co.tz.

What does FDSC do?

FDSC is a Tanzanian development and management consultancy. We carry out evaluations, baseline studies and research, build monitoring and evaluation (M&E) systems, run training and coaching, and give advisory and technical assistance to government, development partners, foundations, NGOs and businesses.

Where do you work?

Across mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar. Since 2014 we have delivered assignments in 23 of the 26 mainland regions, plus Unguja and Pemba. Our office is at Natai Plaza on Coca-Cola Road, Dar es Salaam.

Who have you worked for?

28 assignments for 16 clients, including Solidaridad, Rikolto, HELVETAS, The Nature Conservancy, Trias, the Hanns Seidel Foundation, the Ministry of Finance and Planning and the Sugar Board of Tanzania. Five of them have hired us more than once.

Which sectors do you know best?

Agriculture and agribusiness (coffee, tea, horticulture, rice and sugar value chains), governance and the public sector, gender, youth and rights, and climate and natural resources.

What kinds of assignments do you take on?

Baseline, mid-term and final evaluations, rapid appraisals, M&E frameworks and tools, gender analysis, strategic and resource mobilisation plans, training for staff, boards, cooperatives and farmers, and technical assistance to programmes.

How do I request a proposal?

Send us your terms of reference, or a short note on what you need, where and by when. Use the enquiry form, email info@fdsc.co.tz or WhatsApp +255 786 266 441. We reply within two working days.
