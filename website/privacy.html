Privacy notice.

How we handle the personal information you share with us through this website, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Frontline Development Services Consulting Limited (FDSC), Victoria Noble Center, Plot No. 89, Block 45B, Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam, P.O. Box 105471, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. You can reach us at info@fdsc.co.tz or +255 786 266 441.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site sets no cookies, has no accounts and does not store anything you type. The enquiry form does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with your message ready, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

If you arrive through a link with campaign tags (such as utm_source=linkedin), your browser keeps those tags until you close the tab and adds them to the end of your enquiry, so we can see which links bring people to us. You can delete that line before you send.

What we receive when you contact us

When you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, organisation, email address, phone number and your message.

How we use it

To reply to you, to prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to deliver and manage the assignment. We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists.

Who we share it with

Only the consultants who work with us on your request, the companies that run our email and phone services, and public authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for as long as we need to answer and follow them up. Records of assignments for as long as our contracts and Tanzanian law require.

Your rights

Under Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email info@fdsc.co.tz or write to us at P.O. Box 105471, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

Other websites

Links to WhatsApp and Google Maps take you to services that have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this notice

If the way we handle information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
