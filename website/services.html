Nine ways we help.

We work with ministries, departments and agencies, companies, foundations, international and local NGOs and SMEs, designing tailor-made, innovative and sustainable solutions to organisational, programme, economic and social challenges.

Programme and project management

End-to-end design, planning and delivery support for development programmes and projects.

  • Programme design and theory of change
  • Work planning, budgeting and risk management
  • Technical assistance to implementing units
  • Donor reporting and compliance

Research, monitoring, evaluation and learning

Baseline studies, mid-term and final evaluations, and learning systems that drive evidence-based decisions.

  • Baseline, mid-term and end-of-project evaluations
  • Rapid appraisals and sector studies
  • M&E frameworks, indicators and tools
  • Learning reviews and evidence briefs

Gender analysis and mainstreaming

Gender-responsive approaches built into policies, programmes and organisational practice.

  • Gender analyses and audits
  • Women's leadership and economic empowerment
  • Gender-responsive M&E
  • Youth and girls' empowerment programmes

Organisational development and sustainability

Stronger institutional systems, governance and long-term sustainability.

  • Strategic and corporate plans
  • Governance and leadership reviews
  • Resource mobilisation plans
  • Institutional capacity assessments

Training, mentoring and coaching

Practical capacity building for staff, boards, cooperatives and community groups.

  • Results-based M&E training for MDAs
  • Cooperative (AMCOS) governance and leadership
  • Farmer and outgrower training
  • Mentoring and on-the-job coaching

Data management and analytics

Robust data systems and analysis that turn information into actionable insight.

  • Survey design and digital data collection
  • Quantitative and qualitative analysis
  • Databases and dashboards
  • Data quality assessments

Performance management and development

Frameworks and tools that align staff and organisational performance with strategic goals.

  • Performance frameworks and KPIs
  • Results-based management
  • Staff appraisal systems
  • Strategic plan monitoring

Business management and entrepreneurship

Advisory and training support that helps MSMEs and entrepreneurs grow sustainably.

  • Business development training
  • Entrepreneurship for CSR programme beneficiaries
  • Business planning and record keeping
  • Access to finance and investment

Agribusiness value chain management

Stronger value chains, from smallholder production to market access.

  • Coffee, tea, horticulture, rice and sugar value chains
  • Certification and market access
  • Soil fertility and climate-resilient farming
  • Farmer organisation and outgrower schemes

How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 1Listen

    We start with the client's questions, context and constraints, and agree what success looks like.

  2. 2Design

    We tailor the method: sampling, tools, analysis plan and team, built for the assignment, not copied.

  3. 3Deliver

    Experienced consultants in the field and on the data, with regular check-ins and no surprises.

  4. 4Learn

    Clear findings, practical recommendations and validation with stakeholders, so results get used.
