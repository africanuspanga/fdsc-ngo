## Notes and caveats

- **Field researcher or enumerator (shot 8):** no good free East African photo of an enumerator with a tablet or clipboard turned up on Pexels or Unsplash. `field-discussion-outdoor-interview.jpg` is a stand-in. FDSC's own fieldwork photos, with consent, would be much stronger for the M&E and surveys pages.
- **Outside Tanzania:** five photos are clearly or probably from elsewhere in East or Southern Africa:
  - `women-farmer-harvesting-greens-malawi.jpg` (Malawi)
  - `hands-seedling-uganda.jpg` (Uganda)
  - `women-group-meeting-under-tree.jpg` and `community-meeting-under-tree.jpg` (Uganda-based photographers)
  - `coffee-women-harvesting.jpg` (likely Ethiopia)

  Do not caption these as Tanzania.
- **Visible marks:**
  - The lanyard in `professional-woman-evaluation-conference.jpg` reads "AfrEA". That fits evaluation work, but it is another organisation's name, so crop it out or blur it if needed.
  - One woman in `coffee-women-harvesting.jpg` wears a sports top with shoulder stripes. No logo is visible.
- **Embedded metadata:** `coffee-cherries-hand-picking.jpg` carries an embedded EXIF note, "Copyright 2020. All rights reserved." This is the photographer's camera default. The photo is still published under the Pexels License.
- **Print resolution:** each file is 2400 px wide, which suits the web and inside pages. For a full-bleed A4 cover from a landscape image, download the original-size file from the photo page; Pexels originals are usually 4000–6000 px wide. The Kyimo portrait (2400 × 4800) already covers A4 at about 290 dpi.
