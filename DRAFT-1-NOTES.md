# FDSC: Draft 1 notes

## What we did

**Brand.** The only logo file available was a small JPEG inside the Word profile. We rebuilt it as clean vectors that match the original (the S swoosh, the red disc, the FD/C lettering, the tagline with the swoosh tail passing through "Develo pment"). The S is now a true cut-out, so the logo works on white, on red and on photos. We kept the colours and shapes exactly; nothing was redesigned.

**Company profile.** A new 13-page A4 portrait profile in FDSC red and white: cover, introduction and key figures, about, vision/mission/values, why partner with FDSC, nine services, sectors and approach, a map of where FDSC has worked, six selected assignments, the full assignment record (all 28), clients and partners, and contact (with a QR code to the website).

**Website.** Home, About, Services, Our work (map, selected work and a searchable, filterable record of every assignment), Contact (enquiry form, call, WhatsApp, email, directions) and a 404 page. Tested on desktop, iPhone-size and 320 px phones: no sideways scrolling, buttons big enough to tap, no broken images.

**Content.** We merged the two profiles. The April 2026 profile had two assignments missing from the newer Word profile (HELVETAS Zanzibar rapid appraisal, Feb-Mar 2025; AMDT radio proposal evaluation, Mar 2022), so the record now has **28 assignments for 16 clients**. Text is rewritten in plain British English, keeping FDSC's substance.

Figures we derived from FDSC's own list (please confirm):
- 28 assignments, 16 clients, since 2014 (12 years).
- Work in 23 of Tanzania's 26 mainland regions, plus Unguja and Pemba. (Only Mara, Tabora and Rukwa have no assignment listed.)
- Repeat clients: Solidaridad (5 assignments), Ministry of Finance and Planning (4), Rikolto (3), HELVETAS (3), Sugar Board of Tanzania (2).
- Work by type: 9 evaluations, 6 baselines/appraisals, 4 M&E systems, 5 capacity building, 4 advisory/technical assistance.
